THE BEGINNING

It started like every good wedding story.

Someone had an idea. Someone else said “why not?” And before anyone could stop it, two teams had become two families, a villa had become a wedding house, and a normal weekend had disappeared.

O.K.P — Oru Kalyanam Pole — is our own version of a Malabar wedding: the welcome, the relatives, the music, the food, the teasing, the rituals and the beautiful confusion.

No pressure to be perfect. The only rule is to arrive ready to become family for a day.
