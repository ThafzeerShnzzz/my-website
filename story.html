Our Story

WE’RE NOT ATTENDING A WEDDING.

WE’RE CREATING ONE.

The Wedding Plan.

.

For one night, a villa becomes our wedding house — filled with wedding vibes, decorations, music,dance,colorfull DJ party ,foam through, food, rituals, laughter and beautiful chaos.

Everyone is divided into two families: Bride Family & Groom Family, each with their own spaces, responsibilities and ways of doing things.But there’s more.Everyone gets a character — the annoying auntie, tough uncle, crazy cousin, overexcited friend, family gossip and everything in between. Together, we step into an imaginary wedding universe where everyone has a role and every moment becomes part of the story.

O.K.P — Oru Kalyanam Pole — is our own version of wedding:

No pressure to be perfect. The only rule is to arrive ready to become family for a day.

THE HOST

Created by Isteda Community.

Isteda is a UAE-based artistic community built around people, creativity and shared experiences.

From music and culture to events that bring people together, we create spaces where everyone can participate, perform, connect and simply have a good time.

O.K.P is another Isteda idea — turning an ordinary staycation into something we can all step into, play our parts in, and remember together.
